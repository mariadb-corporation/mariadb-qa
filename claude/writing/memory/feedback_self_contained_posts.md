Never post a short delta. A message that lands after a background job completes carries the full picture again: the finding, the evidence, the options and the question still waiting for an answer. If that is too much to repeat, wait for the pending jobs and send one complete message.

- Never "as above", "per my earlier message" or "unchanged from the last post".
- An interim line is fine only when it carries nothing but progress: "sweep at 10 of 30". A message that carries a result is never interim.
- A task notification is not user input and never answers a pending question. Ask the question again in full.

**Why:** a delta arrives detached from the report it belongs to, and the reader has to reconstruct the thread.

**How to apply:** batch. When several jobs are in flight, hold the response until they finish and post once, whole.
