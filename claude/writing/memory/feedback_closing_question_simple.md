End a turn that needs a decision with a short multiple-choice question. Plain options, on one line. No paragraph on the context, the trade-offs or how the choice interacts with something else at the question.

Ask only a question that changes what you do next. A question with an obvious default is not asked. Pick the default, say so in passing, and proceed.

**Why:** the reader knows the domain. Heavy prose at the decision point slows the reader and adds no information.

**How to apply:** put any long context above, then close with "a, b or c?" and the concrete options.
