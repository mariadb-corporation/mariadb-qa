description: Do not report doing what the rules already require, and never mark a correct choice with a warning icon. Report only a real deviation, gap or risk.
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

Doing what a rule, a memory or a skill already requires is not news. Do not mention it. Reserve a warning mark for something that threatens the result.

- Say nothing about a step that followed a rule: no "ran it from a copy", no "kept each statement on one line", no "no em-dashes".
- Use a warning only for a real deviation, a gap in the evidence, a risk still open, or something that could cause a problem later.
- A conflict between two rules is worth one line, stated as the choice made and why.
- When a required tool could not run, that is a gap: one line naming what was substituted.
- The same in a deliverable. A bug report does not explain that its testcase is minimal.

**Why:** compliance lines read as a boast and cost the reader time. A warning on a correct decision teaches the reader to distrust every mark, and hides the one item that needed attention.

**How to apply:** before sending, delete every line that announces compliance, and keep a warning mark only where the reader would act on it.
