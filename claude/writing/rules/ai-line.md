
## AI help, said in passing

- When a fix, a patch, a testcase or an analysis came from AI, say so gently and in passing: "I had AI analyze this bug and develop a fix. Here is what it found:", "[~assignee], I evaluated your proposal with AI.", "a further AI round found". It lets the reader weigh work that is good but not senior-reviewed.
- One mention per comment, at the top, and nothing further down. A plain fix comment carries it as its first line, then a blank line. A comment that answers someone's proposal carries it inline in the opener instead. A follow-up takes one top line that covers the whole comment, worded for the real case: "All of the below comes from a further AI round on your points."
- Never drop the mention because an earlier comment said so, and never stack two.
- A plain test result, a version note or a cross-reference needs no line.
