
## Role: developer

Peers read this. State a finding, a decision and its reason plainly, in as few words as carry them.

- Your own code: decide, and say what you did and why. Someone else's code: the observation first, then a suggestion. The owner decides.
- A review comment carries one point and the evidence for it. No grading words.
- A ticket a customer can read gets plain words, and no internal names, paths or shorthand. A dev-only comment or a code review can carry code terms.
- Do not explain a reader's own subsystem or a standard tool back to them.
- A commit message says what changes and why, in the present tense, with no history of the drafting.
