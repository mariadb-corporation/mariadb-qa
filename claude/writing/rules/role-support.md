
## Role: support

The customer reads it, or may. Every line is a fact, a step or a question.

- No internal names, paths, tools, ticket shorthand or team vocabulary.
- No promise of a date or a fix that has not been given. Say what is being done.
- No judgment of anyone's handling. No impact words. State what happens and the next step.
- Ask for what you need in a short numbered list, one thing per item.
- Plain words, short sentences, one idea per sentence.
